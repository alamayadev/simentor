---
title: "Get Started with Simentor: From Login to First Action"
description: "Sign in to Simentor, read your dashboard metrics, navigate to key modules, and take your first actions across SKP, Kegiatan, and RKK DIPA."
---

Simentor brings together employee performance monitoring, budget tracking, activity management, and administrative controls in a single workspace. This guide walks you through logging in for the first time, reading your dashboard, and navigating to the modules you'll use most.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Simentor and sign in">
    Navigate to your office's Simentor URL in any modern browser. On the login page you'll see a form on the right side of the screen.

    Enter your office email address (for example, `nama@bps.go.id`) and your password, then click **Masuk ke Simentor**. If you want to stay signed in after you close the browser, tick **Ingat saya di perangkat ini** before submitting.

    <Note>
      If you see the error "Email atau password salah", double-check that your email has no extra spaces and that Caps Lock is off. Contact your administrator if you cannot recall your credentials.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your dashboard">
    After a successful login you land on the **SKP Dashboard** (`/skp/dashboard`). This is your primary command center. At a glance you'll see:

    - **SKP achievement rate** — overall performance score for monitored employees
    - **Active contracts** — open SPK and BAST items awaiting action
    - **Budget realization** — current RKK DIPA expenditure vs. allocation

    Spend a moment reviewing these headline numbers before diving into any specific module. They reflect the current state of operations across your office.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate between modules">
    Use the **top navigation bar** to move between Simentor's modules. The bar is always visible once you're signed in. Depending on your role, you may see some or all of the following sections:

    | Module | What it covers |
    | --- | --- |
    | **SKP** | Employee performance targets, monthly progress, annual evaluations |
    | **Kegiatan** | Field activities, assignments, and the activity calendar |
    | **RKK DIPA** | Budget allocation, disbursement planning, and revision imports |
    | **SPK & BAST** | Contractual documents and vendor monitoring |
    | **IPDS** | IT support tickets and asset management |
    | **Admin** | User, role, and permission management (super-admin only) |

    <Tip>
      The **Kalender Kegiatan** (Activity Calendar) view under the Kegiatan module gives you a chronological overview of all scheduled office activities — useful for planning and avoiding scheduling conflicts.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the SKP dashboard">
    Open **SKP → Dashboard** from the top nav. This is where you monitor employee performance in detail:

    - Review individual SKP scores and verification status
    - Identify employees whose progress entries are overdue
    - Follow up on evaluations that are pending sign-off

    If you manage a team, this view shows every subordinate's current rating and any items that need your attention before the next review cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the activity calendar and budget module">
    Round out your orientation by checking two more areas:

    **Kegiatan → Kalender** — Displays all office activities plotted on a calendar. You can confirm upcoming assignments, see who is assigned to each activity, and spot any gaps in scheduling.

    **RKK DIPA → Monitoring** — Shows real-time budget realization figures per DIPA program, helping you identify underspent or overspent lines early.

    <Tip>
      Bookmark these two views alongside the SKP Dashboard. Together they cover the three most time-sensitive areas of daily office management.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
