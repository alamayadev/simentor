Simentor is an integrated office management platform built for public-sector organizations. It brings together employee performance tracking (SKP), activity and field operations management, budget monitoring (RKK DIPA), contractual document workflows, IT support ticketing, and administrative controls — all in one place. Sign in with your office credentials to access the modules relevant to your role.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Navigate to the Simentor login page and enter your office email and password. Use **Remember me** to stay signed in across sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your dashboard">
    After login, you land on the home dashboard showing your key metrics: SKP achievement rate, active contracts, and budget realization at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your primary module">
    Use the top navigation bar to switch between modules — SKP, Kegiatan, RKK DIPA, SPK & BAST, IPDS, or Admin (visible based on your role).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action">
    Create activities, record SKP progress, submit IT tickets, or generate official documents — all from within the relevant module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Some modules and actions are restricted by role. If you cannot see a menu item or receive an "Access Denied" message, contact your Simentor administrator to review your assigned role and permissions.
</Note>
