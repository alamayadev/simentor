SKP (Sasaran Kinerja Pegawai) is the Indonesian civil service performance management framework. Every civil servant must set performance targets at the start of the year, record monthly progress throughout the year, and undergo a formal assessment and evaluation at year's end. Simentor centralises all of these steps in one place, so managers can monitor completion rates in real time and staff can upload their documents without manual coordination.

## SKP types

Simentor tracks six distinct SKP record types. Each type corresponds to a different stage in the annual performance cycle.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SKP Bulanan" icon="calendar-days">
    Monthly performance records uploaded by each employee. These capture what was accomplished in a given calendar month and are the primary measure of day-to-day activity compliance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SKP Tetap (Penetapan)" icon="bullseye">
    Annual performance target-setting documents submitted at the start of the year. They define the specific, agreed-upon targets an employee will work toward during the year.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SKP Nilai" icon="star">
    Performance scores that are assigned to employees once their work has been reviewed. These underpin the overall evaluation result.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SKP Penilaian" icon="clipboard-check">
    The formal annual assessment records. Once scores are entered, an assessment document is generated to certify the employee's performance rating for the year.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SKP Evaluasi" icon="chart-line">
    Year-end evaluation records that capture the final narrative and structured review of each employee's performance outcomes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SKP Penetapan" icon="pen-to-square">
    The target-setting step at the start of each performance period. Employees and their supervisors agree on and formally record the targets that will govern the rest of the year.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The SKP cycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Penetapan — set targets">
    At the beginning of the performance period, each employee uploads their **SKP Tahunan (Penetapan)** document. This locks in their agreed targets for the year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bulanan — record monthly progress">
    Every month, employees upload a **SKP Bulanan** document covering that month's output. Simentor tracks how many employees have uploaded for each month and highlights those who haven't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Penilaian — formal annual assessment">
    At the end of the year, supervisors record assessment scores. Each employee's **SKP Tahunan (Penilaian)** document is uploaded once the assessment is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluasi — final evaluation">
    The cycle closes with an **SKP Evaluasi Tahunan** upload that captures the final narrative evaluation. The dashboard reflects the updated completion percentage once this step is done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You need the appropriate role to upload or manage other employees' SKP records. Regular staff can upload their own documents; managers and administrators can upload on behalf of others and view organisation-wide statistics.
</Note>
